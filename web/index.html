Design a matching rule. Choose a world. Count the couples.

Each world is a few hundred made-up daters with a hidden answer key: the couples who would say yes, keep talking, meet, and want to meet again. A rule sees only what an app would see, makes introductions for eight weeks, and is scored on how many of those couples it found.

          What a rule can see and return
          
      A rule is a function of v, what the app can see this week. It returns the pairs to introduce. Every table reads "me, then them": s[i, j] is how good person j looks for person i, from 0 to 1. Press a name to put it in your rule.
      
          
      
          
      What a rule cannot see. Who would say yes, who would hit it off, how much anyone truly cares about anything, and who is about to give up. That is the answer key.
